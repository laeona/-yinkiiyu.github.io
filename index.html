Yokai Character Sheet

My D&D group plays in the world of Ryoko's Guide to the Yokai Realms, but the book isn't on D&D Beyond. Rather than juggling paper sheets and PDFs, I built our own web app for the campaign.

It has a step-by-step character builder with the book's races, classes, backgrounds and feats, plus a level-up wizard. The character sheet covers spells, inventory, hit dice and short and long rests, along with Ryoko-specific systems like Tamer companions and Advanced Weapon Mastery. There's also a built-in dice roller, a battle map where you upload a map and move character and NPC tokens, and a day/night theme. Everything is saved in the browser, so there are no accounts or servers to manage.

Built with: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, deployed to GitHub Pages with GitHub Actions.
